About Me

Hi, I'm Joseph Ayele. Currently I am a Senior Software Engineer at Etsy.

I get excited about building things, telling stories, helping people, learning, and taking naps.

Lately that curiosity has been pulling me toward perception — how machines see, classify, and make sense of the world. Perceiving is where I'm putting that into practice: a live camera feed that names what it sees, running entirely in your browser.
